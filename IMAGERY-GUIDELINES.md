# Arvello Imagery Guidelines

## Purpose

Furniture imagery is a primary part of the brand experience. Image treatment must remain consistent across catalog, editorial, and promotional surfaces.

## Image Categories

### Product Images
Use for:
- Product cards
- Product detail gallery
- Search results
- Recommendations

Characteristics:
- clear product visibility;
- clean composition;
- consistent aspect ratio;
- minimal visual distraction.

### Lifestyle Images
Use for:
- Hero sections;
- inspiration sections;
- promotional banners;
- editorial content.

Characteristics:
- warm;
- premium;
- natural;
- believable home environment;
- furniture remains visually understandable.

## Aspect Ratio

Use a consistent aspect ratio within each component family.

Do not mix unpredictable image heights inside one product grid.

## Object Fit

Default product behavior:

```text
object-fit: cover
```

Use `contain` when preserving the complete product silhouette is more important than filling the frame.

The choice must be intentional.

## Object Position

Keep important furniture details away from aggressive crop boundaries.

If the image composition is designed around a subject position, preserve it across responsive states.

## Image Quality

- Optimize images for the web.
- Avoid unnecessarily huge files.
- Avoid visibly compressed images.
- Preserve sharp product details.
- Reserve high-resolution assets for large visual surfaces.

## Alt Text

Meaningful product images need descriptive alt text.

Decorative images may use empty alt text when appropriate.

Do not duplicate nearby visible product names unnecessarily.

## Image Loading

Use stable dimensions/aspect ratios to reduce layout shift.

For large hero imagery, consider priority/loading strategy based on actual page performance requirements.

## Agent Rule

Do not replace a visually correct image treatment merely because another crop is technically easier.
